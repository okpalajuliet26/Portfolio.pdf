GoHighLevel • Conditional logic

Consultation Scheduling & Reminder Engine

Challenge:
Booked consultations were lost to no-shows and manual reminders.
Approach:
Built a trigger on booking that updates the opportunity, tags the contact, sends an SMS confirmation, then runs a reschedule check before a value-building pre-frame message, a 48-hour reminder and a 1-hour reminder, each logged as a note.
Outcome:
Every booking is confirmed and reminded automatically, and rescheduled contacts exit cleanly with no duplicate messages.
GoHighLevel • Multi-branch nurture

Post-Consultation Decision Nurture

Challenge:
Leads went quiet after the consultation, before deciding to enroll.
Approach:
Triggered on appointment status, sent a personalized plan SMS, moved the opportunity to "Decision Pending", then branched: enrolled, follow-up booked, replied, or none. The none lane gets paced 24-hour, 72-hour and 7-day nudges and a team task to monitor replies.
Outcome:
Engaged leads stop automatically; undecided leads keep getting timely support until they move forward or land in nurture.
GoHighLevel • Win-back sequence

Consultation No-Enrollment Follow-Up

Challenge:
Prospects who completed a consultation but did not enroll had no structured follow-up.
Approach:
A four-message SMS sequence (personalized follow-up, reflective nudge, readiness check-in, wrap-up) with waits between each, a task for the team to check replies, and exit conditions for enrollment, booked follow-up, and not moving forward.
Outcome:
Consistent, respectful follow-up with clean exits, so nobody is over-messaged.
GoHighLevel • Long-cycle drip

Paid-Ad Lead Nurture & Re-Engagement

Challenge:
Paid-ad leads were expensive and easy to lose without persistent follow-up.
Approach:
Tag-triggered sequence: intro email within 30 minutes, call tasks, staggered SMS and educational emails, a re-qualification question that branches on the reply, a free audit offer, then warm-lead retirement.
Outcome:
Interested replies create a call-intent tag and a follow-up task; the rest are retired as warm leads instead of dropped.
GoHighLevel • Form trigger

Drawing Entry & Nurture Sequence

Challenge:
Drawing entrants needed instant confirmation and a path toward enrolling.
Approach:
Form submission adds an entry tag and sends instant SMS and email confirmations, then day-based emails and SMS with an enrollment check before every step.
Outcome:
Entrants are nurtured until they enroll, then removed automatically.
Webhooks • Zapier • Integrations

Client Portal Alert & Lead Capture Pipeline

Challenge:
Clients needed instant updates, and form leads needed to land in the right place.
Approach:
An inbound webhook tags the contact and texts them the moment their portal is active. A Zapier flow sends each Google Form response to a Google Sheet row and a Gmail notification.
Outcome:
Zero-lag client communication and no leads lost between tools.
